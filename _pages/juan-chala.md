## Juan Chala

Juan is from Bogotá, Colombia, where he completed undergraduate and master's degrees in Industrial Engineering. A Fulbright scholar, he moved to North Carolina to pursue a Ph.D. in Industrial and Systems Engineering. His research focuses on understanding the resilience of educational systems.

### Beyond the lab

Throughout college, everyone called Juan by his last name—to the point that some classmates never learned his first name. Raleigh was also the first place he experienced four distinct seasons.
