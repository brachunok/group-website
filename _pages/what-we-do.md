  <span>Observation / Models / Decisions</span>

<p class="gd-intro">Communities are systems of infrastructure, social networks, logistics, economies, and governance.</p>

<figure class="gd-image-band">
  <img src="{{ '/assets/img/usgs-aerial-infrastructure.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="Researchers working with aerial imagery and technical equipment in the USGS Aerial Photography and Imagery Library">
  <figcaption>Observation as infrastructure — U.S. Geological Survey Aerial Photography and Imagery Library, public domain.</figcaption>
</figure>

<p class="gd-methods">We combine optimization, data science, machine learning, and simulation to understand those connections, predict the consequences of disruption, and improve adaptation decisions.</p>
