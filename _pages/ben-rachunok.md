## Ben Rachunok, Ph.D.

Ben Rachunok is an assistant professor in the Edward P. Fitts Department of Industrial and Systems Engineering at NC State University and leads the Sustainable and Resilient Systems Lab. His research studies communities as interconnected infrastructure, social, economic, and governance systems, with the goal of making them more sustainable, resilient, and equitable in the face of climate change and natural hazards.

His group combines optimization, data science, machine learning, and simulation to quantify risk, predict impacts, and improve decisions about adaptation, preparedness, and recovery. A central theme of this work is closing the climate resilience gap by identifying and reducing the disproportionate burdens of extreme events.
