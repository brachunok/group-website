## Benjamin Lake

Benjamin is from Charlotte, North Carolina, and has Costa Rican roots. He is the first person in his family to pursue a STEM degree and is completing a B.S. in Industrial and Systems Engineering at NC State. His research in humanitarian logistics uses natural-language processing, machine learning, and rule-based methods to identify patterns in disaster-response data.

### Beyond the lab

Benjamin lived in Costa Rica for two years and attended third and fourth grade there—including during Costa Rica's historic 2014 World Cup run. He also knows his way around basic car maintenance.
