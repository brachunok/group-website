## Suyash Sapre

Born in Boston, Suyash moved to India at age two before returning to the United States to study Industrial and Systems Engineering at NC State. He is especially interested in operations research, supply chains, and the mathematical systems behind how retail networks make decisions under uncertainty. His work asks how optimization, data, and strategy can make everyday systems more resilient—from getting products to stores efficiently to handling disruptions and returns.
