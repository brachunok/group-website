## Caroline Folsom

Caroline is a Ph.D. student in Operations Research at NC State University. She earned a B.S. in Mathematics from Christopher Newport University and a Master of Operations Research from NC State. Her research focuses on access to essential services before and after natural disasters.

### Beyond the lab
