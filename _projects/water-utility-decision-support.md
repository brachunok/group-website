## Project focus

This project develops AI-enabled decision support for rate setting and infrastructure investment in decentralized water utilities operating under drought.

## Collaboration

Ben Rachunok serves as principal investigator. Collaborative principal investigators are Marta Zaniolo (Duke University), Eric Edwards (UC Davis), and Mehdi Nemati (UC Riverside).
