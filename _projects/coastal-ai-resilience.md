## Project focus

This project develops artificial intelligence-driven optimization approaches that connect coastal morphodynamics with decisions about infrastructure resilience.

## Collaboration

Ben Rachunok serves as a co-principal investigator. Collaborative principal investigators are Katherine Anarde (NC State), Brad Murray (Duke University), and Laura Moore (UNC–Chapel Hill).
